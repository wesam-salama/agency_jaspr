# Homepage enhancement brief

## Job and mode
Persuade: help founders and marketing leads understand the six disciplines and prepare a suitable enquiry. Case-study exploration supports this task with illustrative material.

## Direction
The user approved Connected studio, a code-first evolution of the existing dark/red identity and fonts. Lead with “Brand, web and apps. One connected studio.” Keep the eight existing sections and their anchors, four case-study layouts, and email-draft handoff.

## Path
Understand the offering → explore labelled example work → understand the proposed process → select services → describe a project goal → prepare an email draft.

## Contract
App-owned Jaspr components replace destructive reference generation. Services and work render from typed data. Portfolio imagery is visible by default. Native service checkboxes mirror a geometric constellation. Name, email, and project goal are required; timing and services are optional. Dialog focus, interruption, mobile layouts, zoom, and reduced motion are complete behaviors.

## Finish
One batched desktop/mobile inspection and one confirmation at most; share final evidence between polish, audit, and critique. Preserve ../agency_website.html, local assets, and static deployment architecture.
