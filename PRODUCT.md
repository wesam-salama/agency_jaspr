# CR8.Media

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Business founders and marketing leads assessing coordinated branding, websites, apps, and ongoing support. They need to understand the offering, explore work, and explain a suitable project.

## Product Purpose
An English-language agency website whose primary outcome is a qualified project enquiry. The contact flow prepares an email draft; it does not submit or send messages from the website.

## Positioning
One connected studio across brand strategy, identity, web, apps, and maintenance. This is the intended website positioning, not independently verified evidence of agency delivery.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare six service disciplines, explore four project examples, review the proposed process and engagement models, and prepare a brief for hello@cr8.media.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static Jaspr 0.23.4 with Dart and a hydrated browser runtime. Local fonts and imagery. Preserve section anchors, four case-study layouts, and the original ../agency_website.html reference. Name, email, and project goal are required for the enhanced form; service selection and timing are optional.

## Brand Commitments
The user confirmed a Connected studio evolution: confident, direct, precise, dark surfaces, red accent, sharp edges, existing fonts, and the connecting-line motif. Code-first implementation is confirmed. Existing agency names and assets define the website identity. The user explicitly confirmed restoration of the original's full motion language: creative animation is part of the agency's identity and must survive clarity and performance improvements.

## Evidence on Hand
All four case studies, customer names, quotes, results, and timescales are illustrative examples. Engagement terms and service timelines are examples rather than commitments. The user approved verbatim restoration of the supplied HTML copy on 9 October 2026, including its names, quotes, terms and response-time wording, and removal of visible illustrative labels. ## Product Principles
- Explain the offering before asking visitors to interpret the visual metaphor.
- Make a suitable enquiry easy to prepare with a lightweight brief.
- Preserve supplied reference wording and the difference between an email draft and a sent message. Keep the internal example classification; copy approval is not verification.
- Keep keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion paths equivalent in function.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the implemented surface, including contrast, keyboard access, dialog focus, clear validation, zoom, reduced motion, and readable fallback content.
