# CR8.Media

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Business founders and marketing leads assessing coordinated branding, websites, apps, and ongoing support. They need to understand the offering, explore work, and explain a suitable project.

## Product Purpose
A three-language agency website (English, Arabic, French) whose primary outcome is a qualified project enquiry. The contact flow prepares an email draft; it does not submit or send messages from the website.

## Positioning
One connected studio across brand strategy, identity, web, apps, and maintenance. This is the intended website positioning, not independently verified evidence of agency delivery.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare six service disciplines, explore four project examples, review the proposed process and engagement models, and prepare a brief for hello@cr8.media.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static Jaspr 0.23.4 with Dart and a hydrated browser runtime. Local fonts and imagery. Preserve section anchors, four case-study slots and supported layout renderers, and the original ../agency_website.html reference. Sift uses sticky chapters, Form uses split, Frame uses four horizontal chapters, and Fold uses narrative/figures in the statistics family with metrics empty and no results strip. English uses `/`, Arabic `/ar/`, and French `/fr/`, under either a root host or the configured static-site base path. Name, email, and project goal are required for the enhanced form; service selection and timing are optional.

## Product Principles
- Explain the offering before asking visitors to interpret the visual metaphor.
- Make a suitable enquiry easy to prepare with a lightweight brief.
- Preserve supplied reference wording outside the user-authorised Sift, Form, Frame and Fold replacements and the difference between an email draft and a sent message. Keep the internal example classification; copy approval is not verification.
- Keep keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion paths equivalent in function.
- Keep every page and case fully translated, including form feedback and assistive labels. Existing agency Arabic keeps its professional Gulf voice; Sift's international case uses clear professional Arabic. French addresses visitors with “vous.” A language switch preserves the section and an unfinished brief when same-tab storage works, and leaves the current brief intact with an explanation if storage fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the implemented surface, including contrast, keyboard access, dialog focus, clear validation, zoom, reduced motion, and readable fallback content.

Arabic uses document-level RTL direction and locally hosted IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Project names, email addresses, numerals, and other Latin or numeric fragments retain readable direction and identity. The language selector remains visible and keyboard operable without client JavaScript.
