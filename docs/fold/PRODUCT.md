# Fold Clothing

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
International shoppers comparing useful everyday silhouettes, with equivalent English and Arabic shopping journeys.

## Product Purpose
A complete local ecommerce demonstration for Collection 01: Everyday Structure. The main outcome is an informed garment/colour/size choice and a clearly simulated checkout.

## Positioning
Useful silhouettes, considered construction and clear choices. “Clothes that give your day room.” Audience, products and commercial circumstances are fictional studio fixtures.

## Operating Context
Collection browsing, URL filters, four product details with front/back/detail views, laid-flat measurements, size and availability decisions, cart, local restoration, shipping details, simulated checkout and confirmation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Four products, eight colours, five sizes, integer-cent prices and immutable canonical stock/measurement fixtures. Explicit size selection. Versioned browser-local cart. No payment/card collection, external order, message or stock deduction. Canonical basket is Box Shirt Chalk/M + Pleat Trouser Ink/M: $240 merchandise + $12 demo shipping + $0 test tax = $252. Preserve input and valid cart on recoverable failures.

## Brand Commitments
Ivory/ink with restrained citrine and olive, Archivo editorial type, DM Sans interface type, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, manually drawn FOLD signature and a controlled pattern-cut line vocabulary. Square corners, crisp rules, garment-led space and concrete language. The agency shell keeps its independent identity.

## Evidence on Hand
The approved chapter and locked [garment canon](design/garment-canon.md), editable vector identity/packaging and font licences. Generated garment imagery is art direction evidence. Only actual rendered screens/recordings and executed checks establish store behavior; see the separate production and verification records for their final scope.

## Product Principles
- Make the silhouette, fictional composition, half-width measurements and availability legible before purchase.
- Keep image, colour, selected size and SKU synchronized.
- Retain a shopper's valid cart and entered shipping words through recoverable errors.
- Say “Demo checkout — no payment or order will be placed.” at the checkout action.
- Preserve the Latin FOLD signature, readable mixed-script values, keyboard access and reduced motion in both languages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Visible focus, 44 px minimum targets, contrast-checked text, semantic tables and explicit stock labels. Document-level Arabic RTL, IBM Plex Arabic shaping without tracking, logical spacing and isolated Latin/numeric fragments. Error text identifies a recoverable action; status updates are announced and focus moves deliberately.
