## Physical locks

**FL01 Box Shirt.** A compact point collar, exactly five visible front placket buttons and one rectangular wearer-left chest patch pocket with an open top. Short drop-shoulder sleeves and a straight, level hem. Plain back with one horizontal upper-back yoke, no center pleat or center seam. Side and sleeve seams remain fixed. No back pocket, pocket flap, sleeve tab, additional button or logo. M reference laid-flat chest half-width 58 cm and shoulder-to-hem length 70 cm. Pocket sits in the upper third of the torso; front/back body width and hem are identical.

**FL02 Pleat Trouser.** Exactly one forward pleat on each front leg, two side-entry pockets, straight legs and straight hems. Fixed waistband, one visible centered closure button, concealed fly and five belt loops. Plain seat with no back pockets; one center-back seam, one inseam and outer seam per leg. No cargo pocket, cuff turn-up, drawcord, elastic waistband or extra pleat. M reference laid-flat waist half-width 40 cm and inseam 76 cm. Twill remains visibly denser than the Box Shirt poplin.

**FL03 Layer Vest.** V-neck, exactly four visible front buttons and two equal rectangular lower-front patch pockets with open tops. Sleeveless, straight hem, plain unpanelled back with side seams. No back yoke, center-back seam, waist tab, strap, flap or extra button. M reference laid-flat chest half-width 55 cm and length 62 cm. The V finishes above the first button; pockets sit below the third button and end above the hem. Canvas is structured and visibly coarser than poplin.

**FL04 Field Overshirt.** Compact point collar, exactly six visible opening buttons and two equal rectangular chest patch pockets with open tops. Long sleeves with narrow, plain buttonless cuffs. Straight hem, one horizontal upper-back yoke, no center pleat. Side and sleeve seams remain fixed. No pocket flaps, pocket buttons, sleeve tabs, hidden extra fasteners or back pockets. M reference laid-flat chest half-width 61 cm and length 73 cm. Cotton twill remains the same across front, back and construction crops.

The explanatory SVG geometry preserves those feature counts. It is not to scale and must not be cut, sewn or labelled as a production pattern. Imagery uses one front, one back and one visible construction detail for every colour variant. View changes do not author new garment features.
