# Fold — direction and asset decisions

15 October 2026 · authored identity masters, version 1.0.

The approved clothing world is ivory, ink, citrine and olive with crisp rules, square corners and generous garment space. Archivo carries editorial language, DM Sans carries interface information, and IBM Plex Sans Arabic carries shaped Arabic without tracking. The agency's dark/red identity remains outside this boundary.

The manually drawn FOLD signature uses four compact closed vector glyphs. A 10-unit cut separates the terminal of the F's upper arm and shifts that terminal down by 4 units. The other letters remain whole. This single intervention relates to a cut piece aligned for construction. It is not a folded-paper symbol. The F monogram reuses the same path at care-label scale; never invent a second F or Arabic logotype.

The master is 550 × 164 units. Minimum clear space is 28 units, equal to its vertical stem width, on all sides. Digital wordmark minimum width is 72 px; the monogram minimum is 20 px. These are presentation rules checked on screen, not physical printing guarantees. At smaller scales use plain text “Fold Clothing.” The cut stays open at the 72 px test. Ink, reverse ivory and single-colour black/white versions are supplied.

Line vocabulary: a 1-unit solid rule establishes a composition edge; a limited 6/5 dashed line distinguishes an illustrative comparison or graphic fold; 8-unit perpendicular ticks mark alignment. Piece references FL01–FL04 connect to garment records. They are identifiers, not cutting instructions or dimensions. Never add random seam numbers or registration marks to a garment photograph.

The hangtag front uses a large signature and short proposition; its back makes product, colour, size and sample fibre composition easy to scan. The narrow label holds the F monogram and bilingual fictional sample information. The mailer face uses one restrained signature and alignment marks. All are flat editable applications with no unverified certification, origin statement or production material claim.

The source bundle contains SVG mark masters, clear-space/small-size proof, technical garment geometry, packaging layouts and editable HTML presentation boards. The [brand guide](../../../web/assets/selected-work/fold/source/fold-brand-guide.html) is a responsive reading surface with print CSS. Files are authored digitally; no pack, fabric or label has been physically printed or tested.
