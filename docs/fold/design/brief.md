# Fold Clothing — concept brief

15 October 2026 · version 1.0 · authored studio concept.

The approved source is Project 04 and shared sections 1–7 of [selected-work-context.md](../../selected-work-context.md). The working name is **Fold Clothing**, the signature **FOLD**, and the capsule **Collection 01: Everyday Structure**. The proposition is “Clothes that give your day room.”

Fold explores useful silhouettes, considered construction and clear choices. Its international audience is a design hypothesis: people comparing everyday clothing who want to understand silhouette, fabric, fit and price before choosing. English is the content master; Arabic shopping receives equivalent content and functionality, with the Latin signature retained.

The principal job is selecting a garment, colour and explicit size. Questions to answer in order are: what shape is this; what is it made from; how does it measure; is my size available; what is the full demo basket total? Four fictional products, eight colour variants and the canonical stock/measurement fixtures answer those questions consistently.

The selected direction draws from garment construction: a compact, manually drawn wordmark with one displaced cut in the F; solid construction rules, sparse dashed comparison lines and short alignment ticks; generous garment views and legible information. The marks have a documented graphic role. They are not manufacturing instructions. Pattern diagrams are simplified explanatory geometry, never production patterns.

Alternatives considered were an origami letter-symbol, a generic spaced luxury wordmark and a lifestyle-first store. The selected system keeps a particular construction idea visible while allowing measurements and selection to do the practical work. No sustainability, ethical-sourcing, durability or commercial-performance claim is used.

The acceptance scope is editable identity and packaging, locked garment imagery, a bilingual responsive store and a simulated basket journey. The two canonical M-size items total $240.00; $12.00 demo shipping and $0.00 test tax give $252.00. Checkout must say “Demo checkout — no payment or order will be placed.”

Open commercial assumptions: name/trademark clearance, actual suppliers, manufacturing patterns, fit tests, colour/lab-dip approvals, fibre testing, care instructions, production printing, order fulfilment, return rules, shipping coverage, tax and independent translation review. All garment construction, composition, price, stock and packaging content are fictional design fixtures. This work establishes no trading business, client commission or garment manufacture.

The concept direction and garment canon are locked for this authorised local production. [garment-canon.md](garment-canon.md) controls imagery and product content. Completion and implementation evidence are recorded separately by the production and verification records.
