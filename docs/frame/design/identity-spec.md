# Frame Academy — identity specification

Version 1.0.1 · authored 13 October 2026, final confirmation 14 October 2026 · Self-initiated studio project. The approved creative source is the Frame chapter and shared sections 1–7 of selected-work-context.md.

The next frame identifies a course or useful next step. It gives the public website a viewing tool and stays quieter around learning content. The compact Frame name does not replace the canonical name Frame Academy in introductions.

The original custom-drawn wordmark uses an upright F, open r shoulder, geometric a counter, two measured m shoulders and a cut-open e. It is editable SVG path artwork rather than a renamed font. The 514-unit drawing has a 108-unit F cap height, 30-unit lowercase shoulder line, 18–20-unit stems and optically adjusted letter gaps. The primary lockup places the corner symbol at the left and a small editable ACADEMY descriptor below the wordmark. The compact variant is the lettering alone.

The symbol is a 100 × 100 construction with two offset L shapes. Stroke is 12 units with butt endpoints and miter joins, matching the authoritative SVG masters. First path: M14 60 V14 H62. Second path: M40 86 H86 V38. The opposing corners imply a window but never close it. Its orientation is a brand invariant across English and Arabic; navigation arrows are a separate interface concern. This square, blue, offset-corner structure differs from Form's single 3:4 structural frame and uppercase architectural wordmark.

Clear space is 12 symbol units on every side, excluding an enclosing app-icon field. Digital reference sizes are a 24 px standalone symbol accompanied by the name and 160 px compact wordmark (320 px full lockup). The proof sheet tests 24/32/48 px symbol and 160/240 px compact wordmarks and a 320 px full lockup. These are digital inspection limits; physical printing remains unverified.
