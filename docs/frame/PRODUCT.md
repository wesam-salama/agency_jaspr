# Frame Academy

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
Adults learning alongside work, students building a first portfolio and independent creators. International audience, English master and complete Arabic journey.

## Product Purpose
Practical introductory photography, filmmaking and visual-design learning. This is a self-initiated studio concept.

## Operating Context
Responsive discovery website and Flutter mobile learning app. A local demonstration service persists one learner record across both surfaces.

## Authorized canon
The shared sections 1–7 and complete Project 03 chapter in `../selected-work-context.md` govern identity, copy, course records, interaction and evidence. Frame Academy is the full brand; Frame is the compact app name. Master tagline: “Make your next idea.” Brand promise: “Know where to start. Know what comes next.”

## Core journey
Course choice → actual lesson preview → explicit demo enrolment → matching native course → playable authored lesson → practice note → explicit completion → 1 of 6 complete → app restart → lesson 02. Opening a lesson never completes it. Initial progress is 0 of 6. Successful persistence must be acknowledged before saved feedback. Controlled save failure retains entered work and offers retry.

## Content and boundaries
Composition Essentials (`FA-PH-01`), Photography, Beginner, six lessons, 62 planned study minutes, free demo. Light for Motion (`FA-FI-02`), Filmmaking, Beginner, five lessons, 74 planned minutes, preview only. Designing with Grids (`FA-DE-03`), Visual design, Beginner, five lessons, 56 planned minutes, preview only. Produced media duration is measured separately from planned lesson/curriculum time.

No commercial launch, paid enrolment, password, instructor review, accreditation, client relationship, student count or business result is represented. Demonstration persistence and native targets are named according to actual validation. No external publication is in scope.

## Acceptance
Website discovery/detail/enrolment and app learning/overview/player/exercise/progress are implemented in English and Arabic. Keyboard, focus restoration, RTL, mixed-script data, text expansion and reduced motion remain usable. Recorded evidence must prove shared course data, failure/retry, confirmed draft save, explicit completion and continuity after native restart.
