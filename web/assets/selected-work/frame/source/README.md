# Frame Academy editable asset handoff

Authored 13 October 2026; verified final exports 14 October 2026. Self-initiated studio project, local digital production.

The SVGs are authoritative editable vectors/typesetting. Covers embed the approved fonts and owned photograph for standalone portability. Their PNG/WebP exports are actual browser proofs. Identity artwork includes primary/mono/reverse/compact wordmarks, the original two-corner symbol, clear space, app icon and full system board.

The EN/AR HTML workbooks and exercise sheets are editable, responsive and print-ready. Keep source/ and fonts/ and media/ as sibling directories. Serve the folder with a local static server to view or print them. Six course covers share the exact approved fixtures. Composition contains the canonical six-lesson sequence. Light and Grids include five explicitly authored companion prompts each; they are preview-only courses with no produced lesson videos.

The eight PDFs contain 40 pages in total: Composition 7 pages per language, Light 6 per language, Grids 6 per language, and one exercise sheet per language. Final page proofs live in docs/frame/verification/artwork/pdf-proofs/.

media/see-the-edges.mp4 and see-the-edges-ar.mp4 are 30.000-second authored silent visual lessons, 1920×1080 H.264 at 30 fps. Editable scene copy and camera framing live in source/lesson-film.html. Captions and text transcripts are complete in both languages. The eight-minute first-lesson target and 62-minute course total are planned study time, including practice; they are separate from media duration.

The ceramic sphere photograph is generated concept imagery retained as media/composition-source.png. Exact prompt/tool origin are recorded in docs/frame/production/generation-prompts.json and embedded inside the PNG. It is not learner work or evidence of software behaviour. Original licensed fonts and OFL texts are bundled in fonts/.

Rendering commands are preserved in docs/frame/verification/artwork/. They use the recorded Codex Node/Python paths and installed Chrome, ffmpeg, cwebp and Poppler. These environment paths may need adjustment elsewhere. The editable files are usable without rerunning the authoring pipeline.

No commercial launch, accreditation, physical print production, instructor review, trademark clearance or client outcome is represented.
