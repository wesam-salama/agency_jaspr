Frame Academy — Composition Essentials
Lesson 01: See the edges
Authored silent visual lesson, 30 seconds. Planned study: 8 minutes.

[00–04 sec] Where you place an object changes how an image reads.

[04–10 sec] Make the first photograph with your object in the centre. Notice the space on either side.

[10–16 sec] Change the frame around the same object. Notice the space that opens beside it.

[16–21 sec] The first feels steady. The second gives space a larger role. Choose the placement deliberately.

[21–26 sec] Make two photographs of the same object: one in the centre, one toward an edge. Keep the light and compare.

[26–30 sec] What changed? Write a practice note, save it, then explicitly complete the exercise in the app.

Visual description: the same coral ceramic sphere is framed first at the centre, then toward the left edge. A frame moves around the object. Both images are shown together for comparison.

Exercise: Make two photographs of the same object. In the first, place it in the centre. In the second, move it toward one edge. Compare what changes.
Materials: a phone/camera, one object and available light.
Completion: playing the media does not complete the lesson. Save a practice note and choose the explicit exercise-completion action.
